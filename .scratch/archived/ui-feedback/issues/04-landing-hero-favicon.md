## Answer

- **Hero:** the viewport height under the sticky nav, with the nav's logo mark as a large muted backdrop and "RPG Manager" centred over it. A bobbing chevron scrolls to the content (pitch, screenshot, calls to action, footer); with reduced motion it jumps and doesn't bob. New key `home.scrollDown` (EN and FR).
- **Icons**, generated by the orchestrator from the nav logo path:
  - `public/favicon.svg` (ink mark, light in dark mode), referenced first in `index.html`;
  - `favicon.ico` (16, 32 and 48 px);
  - the 8 PWA icons in `public/icons/` (ink mark on the paper colour, inside the maskable safe zone).
- **Checked:**
  - Landing spec 9/9; full suite green with U06.
  - Browser at 1280 px and 375 px: the hero fills the screen, with no horizontal scroll and no console error.
- **Left as Consider:**
  - The hero height hard-codes the nav height.
  - The logo path is duplicated (nav, hero, favicon): a shared logo component would avoid drift.
  - The favicon follows the OS theme, not the in-app theme.
